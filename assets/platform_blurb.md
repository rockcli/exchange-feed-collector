Built a resilient multi-exchange market data collector in Python (`asyncio` + `aiohttp` + SQLite WAL) normalizing live WebSocket and REST feeds from Coinbase (`BTC-USD`), Kraken (`XBT/USD` → `BTC-USD`), and OKX (`BTC-USDT`) while keeping quote currencies explicit. The pipeline pairs capped jittered exponential backoff, a per-venue circuit breaker, monotonic stale-feed detection immune to exchange clock skew, and non-blocking REST gap backfill with idempotent deduplication. Verified with both deterministic offline replay (`177` deduplicated ticks, `1` recovered 7s gap, `15` passing tests under strict `ruff` and `mypy`) and a live 50-second multi-venue capture (`966` ticks, `0.72 bps` mean `USD-USD` spread, `16.39 bps` mean `USDT-USD` basis, `15.1–133.0 ms` live `p95` ingest latency).
